Rise compatibility test

What this is. Rise has limits that cannot be seen from the outside. This page finds out which ones apply to your course. It takes about a minute, changes nothing in your course, and needs no technical knowledge.

You do not need to understand the results. They are for the person who builds your interactive pieces.

  1. Wait for the table below to fill in.
  2. Press each button in “Try these” and answer the questions.
  3. Copy the report at the bottom and send it to Alex.

To put this test in a Rise course, press the button, then paste what it copies into a Rise code block or embed.
